# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Next.js for staff web, React Native and Expo for iOS and Android, and one NestJS API backed by PostgreSQL.

## Users

Veterinary staff work across organizations and clinics. Pet owners use a dedicated mobile app to access records a clinic has authorized them to receive.

## Product Purpose

VetX brings veterinary practice operations and clinical records into a shared platform while giving pet owners a route to authorized records.

## Operating Context

Staff use the web app and a dedicated mobile app. Clinical records originate at clinics. Costa Rican organizations start in Spanish, with English available across the same workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first release initializes the platform, application entry points, language selection, and service readiness. Account access, pet linking, and clinical workflows are subsequent releases.

## Brand Commitments

VetX should feel clear and composed in a veterinary healthcare setting. Prioritize legible type, responsive layout, light and dark modes, and practical touch controls.
